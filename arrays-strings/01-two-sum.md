## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach

I used a simple approach to find two numbers whose sum equals the target.
I checked the possible pairs and returned the indices of the two numbers that add up to the target.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

I tested the solution locally with a normal test case and an edge case before submitting it on LeetCode.
